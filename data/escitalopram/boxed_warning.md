# BOXED WARNING SECTION

## WARNING: SUICIDAL THOUGHTS AND BEHAVIORS

Antidepressants increased the risk of suicidal thoughts and behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients in short-term studies.
Closely monitor all antidepressant -treated patients for clinical worsening, and for emergence of suicidal thoughts and behaviors [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].﻿Escitalopram tablets is not approved for use in pediatric patients less than 7 years of age [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].
