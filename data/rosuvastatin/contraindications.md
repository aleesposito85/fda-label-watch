# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Rosuvastatin calcium is contraindicated in the following conditions

- Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis [ see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

- Hypersensitivity to rosuvastatin or any excipients in rosuvastatin calcium. Hypersensitivity reactions including rash, pruritus, urticaria, and angioedema have been reported with rosuvastatin calcium [ see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
